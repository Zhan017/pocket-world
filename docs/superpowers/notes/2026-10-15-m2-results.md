## Problems found

1. **A correction round can run past the token cap** (fixed by one retry, `a877b22`). Still a risk: the good
   round 1 used 81% of the cap, and two runaway answers in a row would end the corrections.
2. **The world is smaller than the real place.** Fitting the Creta's 3D model to its photo outline gives a
   car at 0.725x its real size in M1's plan and 0.82x in this one: Claude's camera (6 m, 27°, 50°) still puts
   things about 18% too close. You walk as a 1.95 m person in a world built for 1.6 m. The car is the best
   ruler in the frame (its real length, width and height are known), and Claude can't see this mismatch in
   any of its four views.
3. **Alignment errors are invisible to Claude.** At the car's distance Claude's right road edge lands about
   156 px right of the photo's (M1's plan: 235 px), the road about 1 m too wide. The blend view can't show
   it because the photo is painted onto whatever geometry exists, so photo and render always agree from the
   camera; Claude said "already lines up" both times.
4. **Material samples land on the wrong pixels.** In the failed run the grass sample came from the road edge
   by the left kerb, so every grass area was tiled with kerb and shadow pieces (M1's first plan had the same
   mistake). Claude noticed ("grass areas look oddly textured") but didn't fix it.
5. **Trees took their bark texture on the crown** when Claude gave them a bark material (fixed, `6d6c688`).
6. **Thin posts were walk-through** (fixed, `8700860`): a 0.08 m sign post fitted between the body's
   rays 0.3 m apart.
7. **Looks away from the photo's view:** tiled grass shows a checker repeat at mid distance; surfaces seen
   at grazing angles from the camera (the bonnet, the shrub bed) are stretched; the car's photo shadow stays
   on the road; the car's unseen back is plain; sign plates lose their pictogram off-axis.

## Recommendation

**Go, with a short fidelity pass before milestone 3 (overlay cleaning).** Walking works and the place holds
together from every side; what limits the result now is how closely Claude's plan matches the photo, and
every later milestone builds on that plan. In order:

1. **An alignment view Claude can use:** the plan's edges and outlines drawn over the original photo
   (no photo projection), so a road edge 1 m off shows as two lines, plus the same at the car.
2. **Scale from the car:** after the photo fit, tell Claude the fitted car's scale ("the Creta fits at 0.82x
   its size") so it corrects the camera height or field of view, and check it again next round.
3. **Material swatches:** show Claude each material's cut tile in the correction views, and drop a sample
   whose colour is far from the material's stated colour.
4. **Look-back view placement:** keep that camera clear of the car.

Then milestone 3 (cleaning the camera overlays: the red detection box and the driver inset) for raw frames.
